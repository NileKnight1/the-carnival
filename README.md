# The Carnival
A game made with Godot.

You walk around the grounds, play the booths, pick up what they give you, and at the end you fight the ghost that's making the city creepy.

**Main Games**
- **Target shooting.** You aim with the mouse and hit the targets before they vanish.
- **Whac-a-Mole.** Use the mouse for hitting the targets, like the classic one.
- **Pattern game.** You have to repeat the sequence that was played.

**Side Games**
- **Claw machine.** You can get prizes!
- **Arcade cabinet.** A simple game that gets harder with time.
- **Wheel of prizes.** It just give you suggestion, but it may give you a free ticket.
- **Photo booth.** You take a very normal picture, only with a zombie filter on it.
- **Vending machine.** You can get food from here.

**Inventory**
- You can sell food or prizes you get, and you can eat food, and buy tickets with the coins!
  (Currently tickets booth gives you free tickets)

The main games give you a key, a health item, a plushie, you'll use these in the finale.
**The finale.** You fight the ghost in three phases. The first is timing, the second is memory, and the third isn't really a fight.

## Controls
- Move: A-D / left-right arrows
- Space: jump
- E: interact, skip chat
- Q: open inventory
